Watcher

Point the camera. Say what should happen. Get told when it does.

How it works

Describe what you want to catch in plain words and hit Watch. Watcher samples a frame every 1–10 seconds — your slider — checks it against your rules, and logs the moment it matches, with a snapshot. Scan the QR code to get the same alerts on your phone through Telegram. The counters show elapsed time, tokens used and what the session has cost so far.
